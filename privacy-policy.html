Privacy Policy

Cricket 3D PRO (package com.cricket3d.championship)
Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (career rounds, stars, best scores, longest six) and settings such as sound on/off are saved only on your device. We do not upload this data to our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads).

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Permissions

The game uses the permissions needed to show ads (internet access and, where applicable, the advertising ID). It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
